The Whisper plugin is an implementation of the Whisper speech recognition model developed by OpenAI as a Vamp plugin. The Whisper plugin analyses the text in the audio stream and generates markers corresponding to the tokens (words and/or syllables) found. 

- Whisper Vamp plug-in by Pierre Guillot at Ircam (https://www.ircam.fr/)
- Whisper.cpp by Georgi Gerganov (https://ggerganov.com/)
- Whisper model by OpenAI (https://openai.com/)
- Vamp by the Centre for Digital Music, Queen Mary, University of London (www.vamp-plugins.org)
