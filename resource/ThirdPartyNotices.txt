Models
------

The models embedded in the Resources/models folder of the plugin bundle are the
Whisper models developed by OpenAI (https://github.com/openai/whisper) and
converted to the ggml format (with quantization) by the Whisper.cpp project
(https://huggingface.co/ggerganov/whisper.cpp). OpenAI releases the code and
the model weights of Whisper under the MIT License, and the converted models
are distributed under the MIT License.

- ggml-base-q5_1, ggml-tiny-q5_1, ggml-small-q5_1, ggml-medium-q5_0 and
  ggml-large-v3-turbo-q5_0
  https://huggingface.co/ggerganov/whisper.cpp
